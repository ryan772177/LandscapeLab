# CREDITS

**This file discharges attribution obligations that `ASSETS.md` only RECORDS.**

`ASSETS.md` is the intake register: it says what an asset is, where it came
from, and what its licence requires. A row there is a record of a debt. This
file is where the debt is paid, because some licences make attribution a
CONDITION of use rather than a courtesy, and a condition is not met by writing
it down somewhere internal.

**A credits screen is a later unit.** Until one exists this file is the
discharge, and it must ship with, or be reachable from, anything derived from
the assets below. Ruled 2026-08-27.

**Every row here is also a row in `ASSETS.md`.** If the two ever disagree,
`ASSETS.md` is the register and this is the projection — but they should not
disagree, and adding an attributed asset means adding it in both places. This
is deliberately a projection of one source rather than a second list of facts
(non-negotiable 24); the register carries provenance, and this carries only the
credit string the licence demands.

## NOT LISTED HERE, AND WHY

**These are absences with reasons, not omissions.** A credits file that silently
covers only what was easy to attribute is worse than none, because it reads as
complete.

- **Darek Dubiniec, "20 Medieval Houses" free sample**
  (`/Game/Scratch/FabEval/SM_medieval_house_10`). **Licence NOT RECORDED.** No
  licence text came with either archive — the mesh zip held one FBX, the texture
  zip 16 PNGs. "Free sample of a paid pack" describes how it was obtained, not
  what it permits. It is not credited here because *what the licence requires is
  unknown*, and inventing a credit line would assert terms nobody has read. It
  must not be used beyond evaluation until the product page is read.

- **~3.5 GB of other Fab packs.** Same gap, recorded in `ASSETS.md` since
  2026-08-15: the archives contain nothing but `.uasset` files, so terms are not
  recoverable from disk.

- **Quixel / Megascans content under the Fab Standard licence.** No attribution
  clause of this kind; the obligation is a licence-grant question, not a credit
  one.

- **GameDevGary, *50 Free .PNG Heightmaps for Unreal Engine*** — `ASSETS.md:59`
  records **CC0**, a public-domain dedication with no attribution requirement.
  Listed here as CHECKED-AND-OWES-NOTHING rather than omitted, because "absent
  from the credits" and "verified to owe nothing" are different states and only
  one of them is a finding.

- **ambientCG surfaces** (Snow006, Rock026, Rock051, Rock063, Ground037).
  **Licence NOT RECORDED in `ASSETS.md`, and no licence file ships in any of the
  `Free/*_4K-PNG/` directories.** I first wrote CC0 here from memory; ambientCG
  does publish under CC0, but this project has no record of it and non-negotiable
  9 applies to a credits file more than anywhere else — a licence asserted
  without a source is exactly the unverified-derivation class, and asserting a
  PERMISSIVE licence is the direction that causes harm. These are load-bearing
  in the shipped landscape material, so this gap is more urgent than the
  evaluation-only rows above. **Owed: a licence row for each, from the source
  page.**
