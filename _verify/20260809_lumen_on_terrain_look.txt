FINDING 2 — THE MAP EDGE IS VISIBLE, AND FOG CANNOT HIDE IT

From `ridge_wide` (camera 1654 m altitude) the landscape ENDS in frame as
a hard vertical cut against flat haze. Located by per-column stddev over
the mid-lower band:

    x=1792   std 0.1209   terrain
    x=1832   std 0.0196   FLAT — no terrain
    x=1872   std 0.0199   FLAT

so the edge sits at about 95% across a 1920-wide frame. Crop kept at
scratchpad/map_edge.png; it is unmistakable, a sheer boundary with
nothing beyond it.

**AND THE FOG CANNOT BE TUNED TO COVER IT.** `atmosphere_solve.py` on the
shipped values (density 0.0015, half_height 202 m, datum 101 m):

    camera 300 m    8 km ->  0.3889 opacity
    camera 800 m    8 km ->  0.0848
    camera 1200 m   8 km ->  0.0222

Exponential height fog is HEIGHT-limited by construction. A camera at
1654 m sits far above a 202 m half-height layer and receives almost none
of it, which is why the distant terrain still reads as a hard silhouette.
The pinkish distance wash in that frame is SkyAtmosphere aerial
perspective, not the height fog.

So the map edge is a CAMERA-ROUTING or DISTANT-GEOMETRY problem, not a
fog problem. Raising fog density to hide it would fog the ground stations
(which already sit at 0.389 at 8 km) to hide something only the high
cameras can see.

FINDING 3 — THE FOG MAY NOT BE WRONG AT ALL

The clip proposal calls fog "applied, rendering, NEVER TUNED" and
"showing heavy aerial haze at 8.7 km", which reads as a defect. Checked
against physics rather than against the adjective: by Koschmieder,
sigma = 3.912 / V, so an 8 km opacity of 0.3889 corresponds to a visual
range of about 65 km — clean alpine air on a clear day. That is a
defensible value, not an obviously wrong one.

**"Untuned" here means NEVER VERIFIED, not KNOWN BAD**, and the two are
different claims. The value that deserves scrutiny is not density but
`start_distance_m: 1500`, a hard cutoff giving exactly zero aerial
perspective inside 1.5 km where real perspective begins immediately and
merely weakly. That is a candidate defect and has NOT been tested.
