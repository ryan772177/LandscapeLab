# ⛔⛔ THIS DOCUMENT'S CENTRAL FINDING IS WRONG — CORRECTED 2026-08-26 (later)

**THE BASIN IS NOT SEALED. There is no rim, there is no wall, and there is
nothing to cut.** Everything below this banner is kept as the record of how the
error was made, because the measurements in it are real and only the
INTERPRETATION was wrong.

## What the barrier actually was

Every "unreachable" verdict in this document came from ONE path query from the
plaza. A path query has a finite search budget, and a query that exhausts it
FAILS — which is indistinguishable, from a single call, from ground that cannot
be walked to.

The discriminating test needed no terrain edit
(`scripts/city_path_chain_payload.txt`), on bearing 315:

    A  plaza  -> 520 m    FAIL     (partial length 424 m reported anyway)
    B  350 m  -> 520 m    SUCCESS  189 m
    C  plaza  -> 350 m    SUCCESS  373 m

**B and C both succeed while A fails.** A walk from the plaza to the far point
exists — it is C followed by B — and the single query does not find it.
Reachability over a navmesh is transitive when every leg is a real path, so this
is decisive.

## Everything else already pointed here and I did not read it

Each of these was measured, recorded, and explained away:

- The terrain is **93.0% walkable** at the agent bar over the whole window, with
  no continuous band of steep ground at 1 m resolution.
- **Agent-radius erosion up to 2.0 m does not disconnect it** — tested at 0,
  0.2, 0.35, 0.5, 0.7, 1.0, 1.5 and 2.0 m.
- **919 of 925 probe points PROJECT** — the navmesh exists out there.
- The navmesh sits at the **same offset from the ground** on both sides:
  `nav_dz` p50 12.4 cm reachable, 13.4 cm "island".
- The offline model agreed with the navmesh **91.5% on PROJECTS and 48.9% on
  REACHABLE** — a model that predicts the surface well and reachability barely
  is telling you reachability is not about the surface.
- The 24-bearing sweep found furthest-reachable **252–504 m, median 360** —
  a roughly circular limit centred on the query START, which is the shape a
  budget makes and not the shape terrain makes.

**I called the uniform profile evidence of a uniform wall.** It was evidence
that the boundary did not depend on the terrain at all.

## What this invalidates

- **The ruling to cut a ramp is moot.** There is nothing to cut.
- `city/alpine_basin_town_reachable.json` **understates reachability** and every
  consumer inherits it.
- The encounter planner's *"the basin cannot hold encounters"* rests on that
  sidecar's availability figures and must be re-derived.
- The east navmesh extension's *"navigable ground the player cannot walk to"* is
  the same artefact.

## The lesson, stated once

**A NEGATIVE RESULT FROM A SEARCH IS A STATEMENT ABOUT THE SEARCH.** `FAIL` from
a pathfinder means *this query did not find a path*, never *no path exists* —
and the two are only separable by asking a different question, which here was a
relay through an intermediate point. The instrument was believed because it was
consistent, and consistency is exactly what a budget produces.

# The basin rim: what stops the player leaving town

**2026-08-26. Measurement only, nothing changed.**

The player's navmesh island ends near the town. The 50 m probe localised the
eastern boundary to a band between 300 and 350 m; this resolves it and asks
whether the rim runs all the way round.

## The eastern barrier is real terrain, resolved at 0.5 m

    d_m      elev_m   grad_deg   projects   reachable
    322.0    198.36      23.25       True        True     <- last reachable
    322.5    198.73      36.58       True       False
    323.0    199.10      36.58       True       False
    323.5    199.73      51.34       True       False     <- over the bar
    324.0    200.35      51.33       True       False     <- over the bar
    325.0    201.25      41.94       True       False

A **1.25 m rise over 1 m, ~51°**, against an agent bar of 44.765° and a step
height of 45 cm. Navmesh **projects at every point**, so this is neither a
coverage gap nor a `MinRegionArea` artefact — it is ground the agent will not
climb.

### The denominator mattered, again

At 5 m spacing the gradient at the flip reads **35.71°** — under the bar, which
would have said "not a slope problem". At 0.5 m the same place reads **51.34°**.
Recast evaluates at `CellSize` ≈ 19 cm; a 5 m average hides a sub-metre step.
A gradient without its denominator is not a slope.

**Every sampled bearing crosses ground above the agent bar within 600 m.** The

## Two things this does NOT establish

**The flip distances are sampling-dependent and should not be quoted as an
island radius.** Bearing 0 reports 244 m at 2 m spacing and 322 m at 0.5 m —
the coarser probe hits an isolated unreachable sample earlier and stops. There
are genuine dropouts too: 324.5 m reads reachable among unreachable neighbours.
The *first flip along a ray* is not the *boundary of the island*.

**Six rays at 60° is coarse.** A pass narrower than the gap between bearings
would be missed entirely. "No pass found on six bearings" is not "the basin is
sealed" — it is the strongest statement these samples support.
