20260809 — GROUND CLUTTER, FIRST RENDER: NOT VISIBLE, AND WHY

VERDICT: GT_alpine_GroundClutter is LIVE in the engine and does NOT read
in the frame. The density I chose (1.5 per 10 m2) is too low, and the
mesh is too heavy to simply raise it. Nothing here is a build fault.

WHAT THE ENGINE SAYS — R11's direct instrument, after FlushCache + 240 s
dwell with the viewport parked at forest_floor:

    By grass type report:
    (HeightData):            Num components = 1024
    GT_alpine_GroundClutter: Num components =  862, 3448.00 KB
    GT_alpine_Meadow:        Num components =  862, 3448.00 KB

R11: this counts components FOR the type, and the capture path strips
all-zero types (InitializeFrom(bStripEmptyWeights=true),
LandscapeGrassWeightExporter.cpp:1190), so a counted component
NECESSARILY holds non-zero weights. GroundClutter matches Meadow's
footprint exactly. The wiring is right.

WHAT THE FRAMES SHOW — nothing, and the arithmetic says why

    alpine__forest_floor__20260809T055832Z__..._groundclutter.png
    alpine__forest_floor__20260809T062907Z__..._clutter_after_flush.png

At 1.5/10m2 = 0.15/m2, spacing 2.58 m:

    tight crop      ~24 m2  ->    ~4 rocks expected   (absence is WEAK evidence)
    near-ground band ~240 m2 ->  ~36 rocks expected   (absence is stronger)
    full 70 m disc  15,394 m2 -> 2,309 rocks

No rock is identifiable in either frame. Grass blades ARE visible at 2x
magnification, so the grass system itself renders.

THE PHOTOMETRIC ROUTE IS DEFEATED, AND THIS IS WORTH KNOWING

Frame-to-frame comparison cannot settle this on the current world:

    SKY  (should be the control)  mae 0.06469
    MID                           mae 0.05308
    NEAR GROUND                   mae 0.02853

The SKY moves MORE than the ground between two captures taken 30 minutes
apart with identical settings. VOLUMETRIC CLOUDS ANIMATE, so there is no
same-settings noise floor while they are on. The 2026-08-08 before/after
work established a floor of mean mae 0.00298 — that was measured on
CLOUD-FREE frames and does not transfer.

  ANY FUTURE FRAME-TO-FRAME COMPARISON ON THIS WORLD MUST DISABLE CLOUDS
  FIRST, or its noise floor is larger than the effect it is looking for.

THE MESH IS THE REAL CONSTRAINT

Effective cost per instance is about 1,179 triangles (crude LOD1 average
over the disc) against 316 for a grass tuft — 3.7x. So raising density to
visibility is not free:

     per10m2   spacing  instances@70m   M tris   vs R11 grass
         1.5     2.58m          2,309      2.7        0.09x   <- current, invisible
         5.0     1.41m          7,697      9.1        0.30x
        10.0     1.00m         15,394     18.2        0.61x
        20.0     0.71m         30,788     36.3        1.22x
        50.0     0.45m         76,969     90.8        3.05x

RECOMMENDED, NOT APPLIED: trade reach for density, which is R11's own
ruling for grass ("grass beyond ~50 m is ground texture anyway") and
applies harder to a 0.5 m rock, which is sub-pixel well before 50 m.

    10/10m2 at a 40 m cull -> 5,027 instances, ~5.9M tris, 1.0 m spacing

That is denser than the eye needs at foot level, cheaper than the current
70 m reach, and about a fifth of R11 grass.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT SETTLE

"Too sparse to see" and "not rendering at all" are still not separated by
direct evidence. The engine's component count is strong evidence for the
former, but the decisive test is cheap and has not been run: raise the
density hard (20-50/10m2), flush, capture. If rocks appear it was
density; if not, it is a rendering problem and the component count is
misleading.

PROCESS FAULTS IN GETTING HERE, both mine

1. R11's ORDERED STEPS are 1) build 2) grass.FlushCache 3) dwell
   4) capture. I SKIPPED step 2, reasoning that a brand-new grass type
   had no stale cache to clear. The first capture was therefore taken
   before the landscape had regenerated grass data, and showed nothing.
   Substituting my own reasoning for a locked recipe step is exactly what
   the operating loop forbids.

2. I then ran FlushCache and DumpGrassData in the SAME payload, back to
   back, and read "No grass data." — having just destroyed the state I
   was measuring. Non-negotiable 5: a check that consumes the value it is
   verifying verifies nothing. Re-run in the right order (park, dwell,
   dump) it returned 862 components.
